What is an
Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research sheet used to organize shopping links and notes. Its usefulness depends on the source, the accuracy of each entry, and whether linked listings are still available. This page explains how to maintain those records; it is not an official Oopbuy sheet.

The three-pass audit

Source. Variant. Evidence.

Use the same checks for every entry. Consistent records make missing information easier to notice.

01

Verify the destination

Open the original listing and compare it with the saved description. Check the seller, item identifier, and whether the page still represents the same product.

Record: source URL, seller name, item ID, last checked date.

02

Separate the options

A color, size, or material selection may change both the appearance and the price. Give different variants separate notes instead of combining their details.

Record: colorway, labeled size, measurements, selected option.

03

Label what you know

Distinguish a seller's description from your own observations. If a fabric, measurement, or shipping cost is unknown, mark it as unknown.

Record: evidence source, unanswered questions, review status.

Build your own research sheet

A row should answer a question.

When organizing Oopbuy product links, decide what you want to compare before adding more entries. A useful row should tell you which item it describes, what has been checked, and what still needs an answer.

Keep listing facts separate from estimates. If you revisit the sheet later, a dated note helps explain why an old price or availability status may no longer match.

ILLUSTRATIVE RECORD No live product data
Item
Plain crew-neck T-shirt
Source
Original listing URL and seller identifier
Variant
Selected color and labeled size

Chest width and length, with units and measuring method

Cost

Review status
Needs measurement confirmation
Last checked
The date you personally reviewed the listing

Keep a notes column for missing details. An empty cell should not silently become a confirmed fact.

For an Oopbuy shoes spreadsheet

Keep each size system explicit. Note whether a length refers to the foot, insole, or outsole; those measurements are not interchangeable.

For an Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet

Compare like measurements. Flat garment width, body circumference and a general size chart describe different things.

For an Oopbuy QC checklist

Record which photos show the actual selected item. Useful views can include labels, seams, soles, closures and visible defects.

For a realistic cost record

Separate item price, domestic shipping, service fees, international delivery and applicable taxes or duties. Mark estimates clearly.

Direct answers

Before you trust the row.

A spreadsheet organizes information. Check the underlying listing before relying on a saved description, photo or price.

Can I download an official Oopbuy spreadsheet here?

No. This page is an independent research guide, not an official Oopbuy spreadsheet or download service. Its shopping links open CAFANS.

How do I check an Oopbuy product link?

Open the source listing, check that it matches the saved item, identify the seller and selected variant, and record the date of your check. Mark inaccessible or mismatched listings for review.

Which measurements should I record for clothing and shoes?

For clothing, record the relevant garment measurements such as chest width, length, shoulder width, waist, and inseam. For shoes, record the labeled size, sizing system, and any available length measurement. Keep the units and measuring method.

Do QC photos prove a product is authentic?

No. Quality control photos may help you inspect visible details, but they do not by themselves establish authenticity, material composition, or long-term durability.

How should I compare prices across spreadsheet entries?

Record the currency and date, then separate the item price from domestic shipping, service fees, international shipping, and any applicable taxes or duties. Label estimates and avoid treating missing costs as zero.
